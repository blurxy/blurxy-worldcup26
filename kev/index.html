What this can and cannot tell you
  • CISA has evidence each entry was actively exploited, and lists the action to take.
  • Whether it is known to be used in ransomware campaigns, and whether BOD 26-04 requires forensic triage.
  • EPSS (FIRST): a model's probability that the CVE is exploited in the next 30 days.
  • Whether you run an affected version. The catalog names products, not your inventory.
  • Fix-by dates bind US federal civilian agencies (BOD 22-01). For everyone else they are a priority signal, not a deadline.
  • Ransomware "Unknown" means not known, which is not the same as never. EPSS is a forecast, not observed exploitation.
